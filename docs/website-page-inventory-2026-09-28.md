## Archive and participation structure

- `/speakers` announces the 2026 lineup; 2025 remains at `/speakers/archive/2025`.
- All 50 prominent names link from `/speakers/archive`; 38 new historical introductions do not claim unverified attendance years.
- `/gallery/archive` redirects permanently to `/gallery`.
- Volunteer, internship, stall and Open Mic sections live within `/participate`.
- Earlier gallery seasons remain explicit empty states until correctly identified photos are supplied.
