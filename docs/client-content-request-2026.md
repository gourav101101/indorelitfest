### 3. 2026 programme and speakers
- Final or provisional 2026 programme, indicating which entries are approved for public display.
- Per session: date, start/end time, stage/room, title, description, format, participants/moderator and language.
- Per speaker: approved name, role, 80–150-word biography, portrait (ideally at least 1000 px wide), photo credit, website/social links, language and confirmed/provisional status.
- Exact announcement dates if the lineup should remain embargoed.
- Please identify music, poetry, open mic, workshops, book launches and any children's activities that are actually confirmed.

### 4. Photography and festival history
- 15–25 high-resolution photographs: wide audience views, on-stage discussions, books, readers, performances, venue and volunteer/community moments.
- For each: year, event/session, names if relevant, caption, photographer credit and permission status.
- Please send at least one approved wide photograph of **Daly College** and any usable venue map. Current heritage illustration is not a photo of the venue.
- If older editions should appear separately: provide folders named by year, or a mapping of existing photos to their correct year.
- For a festival timeline: approved milestone descriptions and dates. For statistics (attendance, sessions, speakers, reach): exact figure, period and source. We will not invent live counters.

### 5. Testimonials and press coverage
- **3–5 testimonials:** exact approved quotation (roughly 30–60 words), person's name, role, portrait, year and permission to publish. Organiser copy cannot substitute for a person's endorsement.
- **4–6 press stories:** publication name, date, article headline, direct article URL, approved short excerpt and permitted thumbnail/logo.
- Approved media contact name and email if different from the general contact.
- Please specify whether third-party logos, excerpts and photographs are cleared for use. This enables authentic testimonial and “In the news” sections.

### 6. Partners and sponsorship
- Confirmed 2026 partner list, grouped by exact approved tier (presenting, title, venue, knowledge, media, hospitality, etc.).
- Each partner's logo in SVG or transparent PNG, website, display order and any mandatory legal wording.
- Sponsorship enquiry contact and a downloadable partnership deck if public.
- Do not mix past partners with current partners without a year label.

### 7. Registration and visitor information
The five forms are already integrated. Please supply:
- Entry price or explicit confirmation of free entry; applicable taxes, inclusions and any restricted sessions.
- Whether the submitted form immediately confirms entry or requires a separate approval/email/pass.
- Registration closing dates, capacity or age restrictions, and the process for group/school visits.
- Open mic: eligibility, language, permitted duration, selection process, deadline and contact.
- Volunteer/internship: eligibility, dates, time commitment, selection process and any approved description of certificates or remuneration.
- Stalls: sizes/categories, price, facilities, approval process, booking deadline and payment/refund terms.
- Gate opening times, exact entry gate, parking, accessibility arrangements, prohibited items, food arrangements and emergency/help contact.
- Hotel/travel partnerships only if officially approved: hotel name, address, booking link, distance basis, offer conditions and expiry. We will not imply partner rates or included accommodation without these details.
- If a “Friend of the Festival” or other premium package is planned, provide approved name, benefits, price and booking process. Jaipur's package is not automatically an ILF offering.

### 8. Updates, policies and operations

- Approved privacy, terms, cancellation/refund and photography policies as applicable to your event.
- Final visitor contact phone, contact person, response hours and production hosting/domain access for eventual deployment.
- Confirm which sections are highest priority for the first public release and one person responsible for content sign-off.
